\section{Negative results}
\label{app:negative}
\begin{figure}
  \centering
  \includegraphics[width=\columnwidth]{fig_relic.pdf}
  \caption{Distance of the outermost radio peak from the halo centre
  against merger-catalogue time since collision, one point per projection,
  for peaks detected in the GroupPos-centred, density-cut 144\,MHz maps
  (0.3--2\,$r_{500}$ annulus, peaks above 25 per cent of the annulus
  maximum, 600\,kpc apart). The dashed line is a shock moving at a
  constant 1500\,km\,s$^{-1}$. There is no positive relation: Spearman
  $\rho = -0.18$ over the 258 halos with TSC $\leq 2$\,Gyr (projection
  means; halo-bootstrap 95 per cent interval $[-0.30, -0.07]$) and $-0.09$
  $[-0.34, +0.18]$ for the 52 with mass ratio $\geq 0.1$; projections are not
  independent, so statistics are per halo. Every
  projection has several peaks (median 5), so ``outermost peak'' is a
  loose proxy for a relic.}
  \label{fig:relic_null}
\end{figure}

\begin{figure}
  \centering
  \includegraphics[width=\columnwidth]{fig_aug.pdf}
  \caption{CNN with and without diffusion-generated training samples,
  5-fold out-of-fold $R^2$ with checkpoints selected on held-out training
  clusters: root-mean-square error overall and within bins of merger time
  (merger-catalogue TSC); bars are the mean of three paired seeds, dots the
  individual seeds. Overall $R^2$ is unchanged ($0.398 \to 0.404$,
  $\Delta = +0.006 \pm 0.021$). This exploratory augmentation experiment did
  not demonstrate a reliable improvement; because the generator was not
  isolated from downstream test clusters, neither the overall difference
  nor the age-bin shifts provide an independent estimate of the
  augmentation effect.}
  \label{fig:aug}
\end{figure}
