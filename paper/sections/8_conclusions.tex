\section{Conclusions}
\label{sec:conclusions}

We trained convolutional neural networks (CNNs) on TNG-Cluster radio and X-ray maps to infer pseudo-TSC, a merger-score-derived indicator of recent merger activity, and applied the CNNs to LoVoCCS observations.
For clean inputs pooling three projections per cluster, mean out-of-fold $R^2$ was 0.485 for radio, 0.511 for X-rays, and 0.545 for joint inputs.
Radio predictions remained informative within mass bins and after equalizing integrated emission, although correlations between mass, luminosity, and morphology prevent a complete separation of their contributions.

A median of 99\% of unfiltered radio weight arose in sections of gas with $n_\text{H}=10^{-4}$ cm$^{-3}$, including in cold gas.
This motivated restricting the emitting gas to the diffuse environment targeted by the shock model, and illustrates the need to check which gas phases dominate synthetic observables.

Under forward-modelling, five-model ensembles score $R^2=0.203$ for individual 3 Mpc radio observations and 0.406 for X-ray observations.
Combining radio data with noisy mass measurements was promising, but needs confirmation with matched folds.
Adding 1 Mpc radio inputs to X-rays gave no statistically-significant improvement; a matched joint test using 3 Mpc radio inputs remains necessary.
A separate exposure experiment found diminishing X-ray performance gains beyond approximately 50 ks.

Application to LoVoCCS yielded radio predictions for 26 clusters and X-ray predictions for 15.
In the overlapping sample, the rankings correlated at $\rho=0.61$, but radio assigned older ages to 14 of the 15 systems, with a median offset of 0.44 Gyr.
This demonstrates consistency of ordering alongside an unresolved difference in proxy-age scales.

The experiments establish recoverable merger history information in simulated cluster observations.
Inference of physical collision ages still requires independent merger constraints, robust emission and observing models, and uncertainty calibration that accounts for the compressed predicted age range and the 4 Gyr label cap.
