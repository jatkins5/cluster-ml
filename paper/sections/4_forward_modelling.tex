\section{Forward modelling}
\label{sec:fwd}

\subsection{Radio: from simulated maps to LoTSS}
\begin{itemize}
  \item The simulated map is linear emission (summed DSA weight per pixel).
        It is cropped and rebinned flux-conservingly to a 128-pixel grid
        covering a 3\,Mpc box at each target's redshift (1\,Mpc holds a
        median 4 per cent of a cluster's shock emission, 3\,Mpc 65 per cent),
        then beam-convolved.
  \item Flux: the box's own linear emission times one global constant,
        fixed so the sample-median \emph{total} power matches the median of
        the Cuciti et al.\ (2023) relation; the constant is the same for
        every box size and for snapshot 91, and no per-cluster mass relation
        is imposed. An earlier recipe interpolated the arcsinh-stretched map
        and clipped it before summing; that distorted the fluxes (losing a
        median 53 per cent of the power in a 1\,Mpc box, with an added
        trend with \rfive) and is superseded.
  \item Noise must be beam-correlated: with white noise, real-cluster
        predictions ran to 10--27\,Gyr.
  \item Field injection into real LoTSS DR3 fields (209 downloaded, 206
        accepted at 40$'$; 202 at 80$'$, used here) (Bruno et al.\ 2023
        precedent; Bottrell ``FullReal'' tier): real noise, sources and
        artefacts.
  \item Realism, with the metric fixed before the comparison: median
        $|$separation$|$ of four Minkowski functionals over 1--20$\sigma$,
        in mock standard deviations, against real cutouts of the same box
        size. 3\,Mpc linear emission 0.095; 3\,Mpc with arcsinh-compressed
        morphology 0.158; 1\,Mpc linear 0.403; background-only fields 0.64
        (3\,Mpc) and 1.47 (1\,Mpc).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{X-ray: from 2\,Ms mocks to real Chandra depths}
\label{sec:xray}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Mocks: 2\,Ms ACIS-I (Cycle 22) photon counts, placed at a real
        target's redshift ($1/D_L^2$ dimming, sky-area scaling of the
        backgrounds per physically fixed pixel, common aperture) and taken to
        that target's depth with the real data's own count model:
        focused photons (source and sky) thinned to its Cycle-22-equivalent
        exposure $T_{\rm eq}$, particle background at its livetime, and the
        particle subtraction of the real processing applied with the noise
        of its 9.5--12\,keV estimate. $T_{\rm eq}/T_{\rm live}$ is 0.85--1.83
        for these targets; an earlier version thinned to raw time and so gave
        the mocks too few photons.
  \item Status of this model: the exposure-convention defect is corrected,
        with an explicitly approximate scalar background treatment and
        incomplete end-to-end validation. Thinning is exact for fully exposed
        pixels and approximate where the mocks' own exposure varies (chip
        gaps, dither edges); the mocks do not yet carry each real
        observation's roll, gaps and dither footprint.
  \item On the real targets, the robust outer-aperture noise relative to
        the mean rate, real/mock, is 1.12 (16--84 per cent 0.81--1.20;
        the earlier model gave 0.71). One target, A763 (6\,ks), is far
        smoother than its mocks (0.07), possibly from gap filling; not
        explained.
  \item TNG over-luminosity: L$_X$ scale 0.358, from the catalogue offset
        (0.278) times the mass-matched real/mock ratio measured on Chandra
        (1.29); a single global factor fixed without labels.
  \item Galactic absorption per target (small: median 2\%).
  \item Real-data processing: CIAO exposure maps, particle background from
        9.5--12\,keV, Cycle-22-equivalent exposure, chip gaps filled.
\end{itemize}
